1. Arrive

Disagreement is not automatically failure.

In Chapter 11, you learned that shared life requires ways to reason, object, revise, and repair across disagreement. This workbook helps you slow down a public disagreement and ask what reasons, evidence, limits, and shared tasks are still available.

Privacy reminder: You may use the provided case, a fictional example, a public example, a historical example, a composite example, or a low-stakes personal example. Do not disclose private, identifying, or sensitive information.
2. Read the Case

Phones in School

A school district is debating whether to limit student phone use during the school day. One group argues that phones damage attention and learning, so students need protected time away from constant distraction. Another group argues that phones support safety, family communication, translation, accessibility, organization, and learning tools. The debate becomes heated. Some people say, “Anyone against restrictions does not care about education.” Others say, “Anyone supporting restrictions wants to control students.” At first, each side sees the other as the problem.

3. Issue and Claims

Name the disagreement clearly.

Claim: what someone is trying to show or argue for. Public reason works better when the claim is stated clearly instead of hidden inside slogans.
4. Evidence and Warrants

Separate support from the reasoning bridge.

Evidence is the support offered for a claim. Warrant is the reasoning bridge that connects the evidence to the claim.
5. Rebuttal and Qualifier

Make the claim more responsible.

Rebuttal: a serious objection, exception, or limit. Qualifier: language that limits the strength, scope, or conditions of a claim.
6. Joint Task Space

Find the shared problem area.

Joint task space: a shared problem area where people can still work together even if they disagree.
7. Public Reason and Limits

Protect justice, not shallow harmony.

Important: Public reason does not mean every opinion is equally supported. It does not mean harmful claims deserve unlimited platform. It does not mean politeness is more important than justice.
